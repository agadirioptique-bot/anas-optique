Chapter 1

OPTICOR

OPTICOR replaces the slip booklet, the cash book and the Excel sheets. Every pair of glasses ordered becomes an order that follows the production steps until the client picks it up; every dirham collected, every frame taken out of stock and every expense is recorded in the same place. The optician sees what is happening in all his stores in real time.

There are two kinds of accounts:

The secretary secretary

She works in one store only. She welcomes clients, takes orders (prescription, frame, lenses, deposit), moves orders forward, collects the balance and hands over the glasses. She sees the stock of her store (quantities, never costs).

The super admin super admin

The optician (or his partner). He sees every store and the lab, the workshop, the figures (revenue, cash collected, charges, net result), moves stock between stores, manages supplier purchases, charges and every setting. Only he can cancel an order or grant a discount.

Security does not depend on the screenThese rules are enforced by the database itself, not only by the menus: even by typing an address by hand, a secretary never gets a cost, a purchase or a charge, nor the orders of another store. If she opens a restricted page, she sees Access denied.
Chapter 2

Getting started

Receiving your access

Nobody creates their own account: the super admin invites each person from Settings › Users. The person receives an invitation email (in French: « Votre accès à OPTICOR ») with a button to choose a password. The link works once; if it has expired, the admin sends the invitation again.

Signing in

  • The secretary lands directly on Orders; the super admin on the Dashboard.
  • Forgot password? sends an email to choose a new one.
  • If the admin changes a person's store or role, that person is signed out ("Your session has expired"): signing in again gives the new access.
  • A deactivated account can no longer sign in ("This account is disabled").
  • The FR / EN switch (top right) changes the language; each person can also choose it in their menu.

The main screen

The menu on the left

The + New order button is always at the top. Below it, the pages you have access to: the secretary sees Orders, Clients, Stock; the admin also sees Dashboard, Workshop, Purchasing, Charges, Settings. Orange badges show what is waiting: orders to process in the workshop, charges to approve. Collapse menu folds it into icons.

The top bar

Store: the secretary sees her store (fixed); the admin chooses "All stores" or one store (see B2). Search: finds a client or an order. Bell: live notifications. Initials: the personal menu (language, light/dark theme, sign out).

Quick search Ctrl + K

From any page, Ctrl+K (or a click on the search bar) opens the search. It finds clients by name or phone, and orders by number, written any way: 1215, 01215, mrf-1215 or MRF-01215. Arrow keys to choose, Enter to open. The secretary only finds the orders of her store.

On a phone

OPTICOR also works on phones and tablets, with nothing to install: open the address in the browser.

  • The left menu becomes a bar at the bottom with a large + button for a new order; More opens the rest.
  • On order cards, a next-step button (e.g. Send to workshop) replaces drag and drop.
  • Windows (payment, new client…) open full screen, with the main button at the bottom.
  • For the prescription photo, the phone offers the camera directly.
Chapter 3

The journey of a pair of glasses

Everything in OPTICOR revolves around this journey. An order goes through five steps, always in this order; at every step the system knows where the glasses are, how much the client has paid and what is left to collect.

ConfirmedThe slip is entered, the deposit collected, the frame taken out of stock.
→
In workshopThe order has gone to fitting (lenses ordered, frame sent).
→
PreparedThe glasses are fitted and checked, ready to go to the store.
→
In storeThe glasses are waiting for the client, who is notified (WhatsApp).
→
DeliveredThe client has paid the balance and picked up the glasses.
Cancelled — only the super admin can cancel, at any step (even after delivery, for a return). The frame goes back into stock; a refund can be recorded.
Late — if an order stays too long at the same step (delays set in Settings › Notifications), its card turns red and a notification is sent.

What happens at each step

StepWhoWhat the system does
ConfirmedSecretary (or admin) — New orderChecks that the slip number is not already used in the store, that the frame is in stock and that the total covers at least its price. Records the prescription, the deposit (even 0 DH) and takes the frame out of stock. The order number is created: store prefix + slip number, e.g. MRF-01215.
In workshopSecretary or admin — Send to workshopThe order appears in the Workshop queue (admin). The "Workshop" counter in the menu counts confirmed and in-workshop orders.
PreparedUsually the workshop (admin) — Mark as preparedThe glasses are ready; they leave the workshop counter.
In storeSecretary at the front desk — Arrived in storeThe Notify on WhatsApp button appears (message ready to send to the client). The order shows in the "Ready for pick-up" widget of the dashboard.
DeliveredSecretary — Collect the balance, then Collect and deliverOnly possible when the balance due is 0: the balance is collected in the same window. The delivery counts towards the client's loyalty (see A4).

Money and stock, in one sentence

  • Total = what the client pays (frame + lenses + treatments); paid = the sum of the payments; balance due = total − paid − discount. The balance is shown in orange everywhere until it reaches 0.
  • The frame leaves the stock as soon as the order is confirmed (it is reserved for this client) and comes back if the order is cancelled. Stock is never changed "by hand": every movement (purchase, sale, transfer, count, damage, cancellation) is recorded and can be looked up.
Part A

The secretary

The everyday work at the front desk: welcoming the client, entering the order, moving the glasses forward, collecting and delivering. Everything below also applies to the super admin, who can do the same in every store.

A1 · Orders secretary admin

Orders: board and list

The Orders page is the secretary's home page. It has two views, chosen at the top: Board and List.

The board (view by step)

One column per step: Confirmed, In workshop, Prepared, In store, Delivered (deliveries of the last 7 days). The number at the top of each column is its order count. Each card shows:

  • the order number (click it to open the order);
  • the client's name, the WhatsApp icon if the client has WhatsApp, the glasses icon if it is the client's own frame;
  • the total and the Due … DH badge (or "Paid");
  • how many days it has been at this step — in red when it is late.

Moving an order forward: drag the card into the next column (with the mouse, or with the keyboard using the ⋮⋮ handle: Space, arrows, Space). On a phone, use the card's next-step button (e.g. Send to workshop). An order moves one step at a time; it only becomes Delivered through the payment window (A3).

The list

Every order in a table: number, client, date, status, total, balance. Filters: status, period (7, 30, 90 days, one year), Balance due > 0 to see who still owes money, Show cancelled. The search box filters by client, phone or number. A click on a row opens the order.

TipAt the end of the day, the List view with "Balance due > 0" gives the list of clients to follow up.
A2 · New order secretary admin

Creating a new order

The + New order button (left menu, or + on a phone). The order is entered in 3 steps; on the right, a summary fills in as you go (client, prescription, products, amounts). Next moves to the next step when everything is correct, otherwise the fields to fix are shown in red; Previous goes back without losing anything.

Step 1 — The slip and the client

  1. Slip number: the number printed on the paper slip booklet. The store prefix is already shown (MRF-). OPTICOR checks it at once: "Number available" in green, or "This slip number already exists in this store" with the order concerned. A number can never be used twice in the same store (the admin is notified if someone tries).
  2. Existing client: type the name or the phone number; recent clients are listed without typing anything. A click selects the client. A yellow banner flags a loyal client; an "Approved discount" banner appears if the optician has granted one (see step 3).
  3. New client: choose New client and enter the name and phone number (required), and tick whether the client has WhatsApp. A phone number belongs to one client only: if it already exists, OPTICOR says so and offers the existing client. It also points out names that look like existing clients, to avoid duplicates.
Clients are shared by every storeA client who came to Anfa is found in Maârif: their history and loyalty add up.

Step 2 — Measurements (the prescription)

FieldWhat to enter
SPH (sphere)The main correction, in dioptres, in steps of 0.25 (e.g. −1.75; +2.00). The − / + buttons add or remove 0.25.
CYL (cylinder)The astigmatism correction (often negative), in steps of 0.25. Empty if there is none.
AXISThe orientation of the astigmatism, from 0 to 180°. Required as soon as there is a cylinder.
ADD (addition)For near-vision / progressive lenses: the extra correction for reading.
Copy RE → LECopies the right eye to the left eye when both are close; then fix what differs.
Reuse the last measurementsFor an existing client: brings back the prescription of their last order.
PD, fitting height, vision type, doctor, date, photoPupillary distance for each eye, fitting height, distance / near / distance + near vision, doctor's name, prescription date, prescription photo. The optician decides in Settings › Custom fields which ones are hidden, optional or required, and can add his own.

OPTICOR refuses impossible values (e.g. a sphere that is not a multiple of 0.25, an axis without a cylinder) and explains it under the field. The prescription photo can be taken with the phone or added later from the order page (image or PDF).

Step 3 — Products and payment

The frame

Frames are managed by price category (Catégorie A, B, C…), not model by model. Each card shows the name, the sale price and how many are in stock in this store. A category at 0 is greyed out ("Out of stock": the admin can send some from another store). If the client brings their own frame, tick Client's own frame: nothing leaves the stock, and the glasses icon will show everywhere on this order. The tabs above also give contact lenses and accessories (category + quantity).

The lenses

Choose the lens type (single vision, progressive, bifocal) and the treatments (anti-reflective, blue light, photochromic, hard coat, tinted, polarized… — several possible). The "details" field is for specifics (index, brand). Lenses have no separate price: their price is included in the total.

Total, discount and deposit

  • Order total: the price given to the client for everything. It must be at least the price of the chosen frame.
  • Approved discount: if the optician has prepared a discount for this client, tick it to apply it; it is then used up (it works only once).
  • Deposit: the 30%, 50%, 100% buttons calculate it, or type an amount (0 is allowed). Choose the payment method: cash, card, bank transfer.
  • The balance due shows at once; it will be collected at pick-up.

Confirming

When you confirm, everything is saved in one go: order, client, prescription, deposit and stock movement. If something changed in the meantime (the last frame has just been sold, the slip number has just been taken), nothing is saved and a message explains what to fix.

A3 · Order page secretary admin

The order page: follow up, collect, deliver

It opens with a click on an order number (board, list, search, client page, notification). It shows:

  • At the top: the number, the status, the client (phone, WhatsApp), and the button for the next step (Send to workshop, Mark as prepared, Arrived in store, then Collect the balance).
  • The step timeline with the date of each step.
  • The prescription (RE / LE), the additional information and the prescription photo (add, replace, open).
  • The products: frame (category or "Client's own frame"), lens type, treatments.
  • Payments: each payment with date, method and person; the total, paid, discount and balance. Add a payment records an intermediate payment (never more than the balance).
  • History: who did what and when.

Notifying the client

When the order is In store, the Notify on WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message ("Hello …, your glasses are ready…") to the client's number.

Collecting and delivering

The client comes for their glasses: Collect the balance opens a window with the balance due already filled in. Choose the payment method, then Collect and deliver: the payment is recorded and the order becomes Delivered in the same move. An order cannot be delivered while money is still owed.

Made a mistake?The secretary cannot go back or cancel: she asks the optician, who can go back one step or cancel (with a reason), see B4.
A4 · Clients secretary admin

Clients

The list holds every client of every store. The search finds by name or phone; sort by name, by most recent visit or by number of orders. The segments at the top:

SegmentMeaning
New0 or 1 order (not cancelled).
RegularAt least 2 orders, not loyal yet.
Loyal ★Has reached the number of delivered orders set by the optician (Settings › Loyalty, 5 in the example), all stores together. Becoming loyal is automatic and the team gets a notification.

The client page

  • Contact details (call, message on WhatsApp), segment, date of the first order, number of stores visited.
  • Their orders: the secretary sees the total count, but the details only of those from her store.
  • Vision over time: a chart of the RE/LE sphere across orders.
  • New order for this client opens the order form with the client already selected.
  • Edit corrects the name, phone or WhatsApp.
A5 · Stock secretary

The stock of her store

The secretary sees the stock of her store, read only: she knows what she can sell, without seeing any purchase price.

  • At the top: number of tracked categories, number of items in stock, number of categories below the threshold.
  • Filters: product type (frames, contact lenses, accessories), search, Low stock only.
  • The quantity pill: red below the minimum threshold, amber at the threshold, neutral above. The threshold is set by the optician.

The history of a category

A click on a row (or the clock icon) opens its history: purchase received, sale (with the order number), cancellation, transfer received or sent, count, damage — with the date, the person and the quantity left after each movement. Useful to understand a difference.

A category is at 0?Ask the optician for a transfer: he sends frames from another store or from the lab, and they appear in your stock right away.
A6 · Notifications secretary admin

Notifications

The bell at the top right shows the number of unread notifications. It updates live: a new notification shows a short message at the bottom of the screen (with Open), without reloading the page.

  • A click on a notification marks it as read and opens the right page directly (the order, the client, the filtered stock…).
  • Mark all as read clears the counter; See all notifications opens the full page, with an All / Unread filter.

For her store, the secretary receives:

NotificationWhen
Low stockA category drops to its minimum threshold (once each time it goes below).
Late orderAn order stays too long at the same step (once per order and step).
New loyal clientA client of the store becomes loyal at their latest delivery.

Restricted pages

Part B

The super admin

The optician runs the whole business: he sees every store, follows production in the workshop, manages stock between stores, supplier purchases and expenses, and sets how the application works. He can also do everything a secretary does (Part A), in any store.

B1 · Dashboard admin

The dashboard

The filters (at the top)

  • Period: Today, 7 days, This month, Last month, This year, or Custom (from… to…). Days follow Casablanca time.
  • Store: all stores, one store or the lab (by default, the one chosen in the top bar).
  • Compare: with the same period the previous month (vs M−1), the previous year (vs Y−1), or none. Each card then shows the change in % (green up, red down) and a small curve.

The filters are part of the page address: you can bookmark "this month, Maârif" or send it to a partner.

The indicators

IndicatorWhat it measures
RevenueThe sum of the totals of the orders created in the period, cancelled orders excluded. What was sold, paid or not.
Cash collectedThe money actually received in the period (deposits, payments, balances), refunds deducted. It is the main card on "Today": it should match the day's cash.
ChargesThe approved expenses of the period (rent, salaries, supplier payments…). With one store selected: only its charges; "General" charges count only under "All stores".
Net resultRevenue − charges: the result of the period.
Orders / average basketNumber of non-cancelled orders, and revenue divided by that number.
Left to collectWhat clients still owe on the orders of the period that are not delivered yet.
Owed to suppliersWhat is left to pay on all purchases, as of today.
Discounts, cancellation rate, damageTotal discounts given, share of orders cancelled, number of frames recorded as damaged.
Loyal clients, repeat rateNumber of loyal clients, and share of the clients served who already have at least 2 delivered orders.
Workshop timeAverage time from the order to its arrival in store, and to delivery.

Charts and widgets

  • Revenue over time (by hour, day or month depending on the period), with the comparison period as a dotted line.
  • Revenue and result by store, clients served, sales by frame category, lens type and treatment, busiest hours (which day and hour orders are placed), workshop lead times.
  • At the bottom: Ready for pick-up (glasses in store, and for how many days), Pending charges, Low stock — each with a link to the page concerned.
B2 · Store selector admin

The store selector

At the top left, the admin chooses what he looks at: All stores, one store, or the lab. The choice is kept from one visit to the next and applies to Orders, the Workshop, the Stock tab, and is the default filter of the Dashboard. With "All stores", each order card shows its store. A new order is always created in a specific store (chosen in step 1).

B3 · Workshop admin

The workshop

The workshop is the fitting to-do list: every Confirmed, In workshop and Prepared order, oldest first. At the top, the summary (to send, in progress, ready, late) and filters by step.

Each card gives everything needed to fit the glasses without opening the order: the prescription (RE / LE), the frame (category or "Client's own frame"), the lens type and treatments, the store, the age of the order. Late cards are red. The large button moves one step forward: Send to workshop → Mark as prepared → Arrived in store (the order then leaves the workshop and shows "In store" for the secretary).

The "Workshop" counter in the menuIt counts confirmed and in-workshop orders: the work still to do. A prepared pair leaves it.
B4 · Orders and clients admin

What only the admin can do on orders and clients

  • Go back: undoes a step passed by mistake (down to "Confirmed"; never from "Delivered").
  • Free discount: a commercial gesture on this order, before delivery, at most the balance due (never below the frame price, and not on top of a client discount).
  • Cancel the order: at any step (even delivered, for a return).

Cancelling asks for a reason (client changed their mind, measurement error, frame unavailable, delay too long, other) and a note. A refund can be recorded (at most what was paid) with its method. The frame goes back into stock automatically. An order is never erased: it stays visible as "Cancelled" ("Show cancelled" filter).

The discount prepared for a client

On a client's page, Approve a discount records a discount (in % or in DH, with a note) for their next order. The secretary sees it as a banner in step 1 and applies it in step 3; once used, it disappears. The admin can also remove it. This is how the secretary can give a discount without having the right to decide one.

B5 · Stock admin

Stock: transfers, counts, damage, thresholds

The admin sees the stock of each location (tabs at the top). The minimum threshold of each category is edited directly in the "Threshold" column: below it, the quantity turns red, the category goes into "Low stock" and a notification is sent. Stock is never changed by typing a number: one of these actions is used, each recorded in the history.

ActionWhen to use it
TransferSend items from one location to another (e.g. from the lab to Anfa). Several categories at once; you cannot send more than there is. The outgoing and incoming movements are recorded together.
Adjust to countAfter an inventory: enter the counted quantity and a reason (physical count, entry error, item found…). OPTICOR records the difference.
Damage or lossA damaged or lost frame: quantity and a required reason. It leaves the stock and counts in the dashboard's "Damaged frames" indicator.
HistoryEvery movement of the category at this location, with the stock after each movement.

Stock coming from suppliers enters through Purchasing (B6): each purchase received adds its items to the receiving location.

B6 · Purchasing admin

Purchasing and suppliers

At the top: the total owed to suppliers, the number of unpaid purchases, the total bought. The table gives, for each purchase, the date, supplier, receiving location, total, paid and balance (badge). Filters by supplier and by status.

Recording a purchase

  1. Supplier (or create one), Received at (the store or lab receiving the goods), date, invoice reference.
  2. Stock lines: category + purchase unit price + quantity → line total. These items go into the stock of the receiving location.
  3. Lens lines (Add lenses): a description and an amount, no stock (lenses are ordered for clients).
  4. Payment now: 100%, 30%, 50%, another amount, or nothing. The total is calculated automatically.

A purchase and its payments

Add a payment records a payment to the supplier (date, method, note), never more than the balance. Each supplier payment automatically creates a charge "Supplier purchase" in Charges, read only (it is corrected from the purchase, not from the charges): the dashboard's expenses are always complete.

The Suppliers tab lists the suppliers with what is owed to each. You can edit them, archive a supplier you no longer work with (it stays in the history), or delete a supplier never used.

B7 · Charges admin

Charges

Charges are all the expenses: rent, salaries, electricity, internet, deliveries, remade lenses, supplier payments… They feed the dashboard's "Charges" and "Net result" indicators. Filters: period, location, category, status; the total of the selection is shown.

Where charges come from ("Source" badge)

SourceExplanation
ManualEntered with New charge: amount, date, category, location (a store, the lab or "General" for what concerns the whole business), a required reason, and optionally a linked order (e.g. a lens remade for order MRF-01204).
Supplier paymentCreated automatically by a purchase payment (B6). Read only, with a link to the purchase.
RecurringCreated every month by a recurring charge (e.g. rent on the 1st). It arrives "To approve".

Charges to approve

Every day, OPTICOR prepares the month's recurring charges (rent on the 1st, internet on the 5th…). They arrive at the top of the page, with a badge on "Charges" in the menu and a notification. Approve makes them count in the figures; you can correct the amount first (e.g. an electricity bill that varies). Until a charge is approved, it does not count.

Edit, delete, history

The "…" menu of a charge lets you edit it, delete it (it stays visible with "Show deleted" and can be restored) and see its history: every change is kept with its author, the old and the new value.

The Recurring tab manages these templates: label, amount, category, location, day of the month, active or not. Deactivating a template stops the next charges without touching the past ones.

B8 · Settings admin

Settings

Everything that sets how OPTICOR works, in the left menu of the Settings page.

Lists

The choices offered in the menus: payment methods, lens types, treatments, vision types, cancellation reasons, stock adjustment reasons, charge categories. Each item has a French and an English label; drag to reorder. An item already used cannot be deleted: it is archived (it disappears from the choices but stays in old orders). The number of uses is shown.

Products

The types (frames, contact lenses, accessories) and their categories with the sale price (the one shown to the secretary). Add a category, change its price (past orders keep their price), archive a category no longer sold. The low-stock thresholds per store are set here too.

Custom fields

For each field (PD, fitting height, vision type, doctor, prescription date and photo…): hidden, optional or required. You can add your own fields (text, number, date, list). A required field blocks step 2 until it is filled in.

Stores and lab

Create a store with its name and its prefix (3 letters, e.g. MRF) that starts all its order numbers. The prefix is locked from the first order, so numbers never change. A closed store is deactivated (its history remains). The lab is a stock location without orders.

Users

  1. Invite: name, email, role (secretary or super admin) and, for a secretary, her store. The person receives the invitation email.
  2. Change a person's role or store: they will have to sign in again.
  3. Deactivate: the person can no longer sign in (their past actions keep their name). Resend invitation if they did not receive the email or let it expire.
SafeguardsYou cannot change your own role or deactivate your own account, and there is always at least one active super admin.

Charge locations

Each store and the lab have their location (created automatically); General is for shared expenses (head office salaries, accountant…). You can add other locations (e.g. "Vehicle") and archive them. Reminder: on the dashboard, "General" charges count only under "All stores".

Loyalty

The number of delivered orders from which a client becomes loyal. If you lower it, the clients who already reach it become loyal at once (a notification says how many).

Notifications

For each step, the number of days after which an order is late: its card turns red and a "Late order" notification is sent (once per order and step).

Frequent questions

"This slip number already exists in this store."

The number has already been used. Check the booklet: either it is a typo, or the slip has already been entered (the existing order is shown, open it). The optician is notified of the attempt.

"Not enough stock in this store. Ask for a transfer."

The last frame of this category has just been sold. Choose another category or ask the optician for a transfer.

I cannot deliver the order

Money is still owed: Collect the balance then Collect and deliver collects the balance and delivers at the same time.

I chose the wrong step / the order must be cancelled

Ask the optician: he can go back one step, or cancel with a reason and a refund.

"Your session has expired."

Your role or your store has been changed: sign in again to get your new access.

"Access denied"

The page is reserved for the optician. Go back to Orders with the button offered.

A client has changed phone number

Open their client page, Edit, and correct the phone number. Do not create a new client: you would lose their history and loyalty.

The day's figure does not match the cash register

Compare the cash with Cash collected (not Revenue) on "Today" and the right store. Revenue counts what was sold, even unpaid; Cash collected counts the money received.

Good practices

  • Enter the order in front of the client, with the paper slip: the slip number and phone are checked right away.
  • Always search for the client before creating a new one.
  • Add the prescription photo: it avoids reading mistakes in the workshop.
  • Move orders forward when they actually change step: late alerts and the dashboard's lead times depend on it.
  • For the optician: approve recurring charges at the start of each month, count the stock regularly, keep the thresholds up to date.
